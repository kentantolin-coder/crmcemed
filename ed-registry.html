Measures & definitions

Total ED visitsAll patient consultations in the selected year.
Admission rateAdmitted ÷ total visits × 100.
ED mortality rateDied in ED ÷ total visits × 100. Deaths are not counted twice as admissions.
HAMA ratePatients leaving against medical advice ÷ total visits × 100.
Transfer-out rateTransferred to another service or facility ÷ total visits × 100.
Transferred toRequired when the disposition is Transferred. Enter the receiving service or facility; it appears in the record, the census and the printed census, and becomes a suggestion next time.
Print this date onlyOn the Census tab. Opens a separate landscape sheet for the chosen date — header, counts, one row per visit, and a signature line — leaving the rest of the page out.
High-acuity shareESI 1–2 patients ÷ total visits × 100.
Period selectionDaily, weekly, monthly, yearly, or All dates. Use the ‹ › buttons to step one period at a time. Weeks run Monday to Sunday. The report cards compare the selected period with the two preceding ones.
Resident in chargeThe physician who handled the patient, chosen from the nine-name roster. A name stored from an earlier version is kept so its records stay complete, and Clean up resident names maps it onto the roster. The Residents tab counts visits, admissions, deaths, trauma, duty dates and the resident's most common diagnosis.
Resident roster3rd year: Dr. Biruar, Dr. Agao-Agao. 1st year: Dr. Acob, Dr. Agustin, Dr. Sorupia, Dr. Torino, Dr. Guinzon, Dr. Usodan, Dr. Baraguir. These nine are the only choices in the list.
Clean up resident namesOn the Patient Records page. Finds stored names that are not in the roster — for example a name typed before the dropdown existed — and asks you to map each one onto a roster name. Nothing changes until you confirm the list.
Consultant on dutyA separate list from the residents. Add names on the Residents tab; they appear in the encoder and in the Consultants on duty table with patients seen, dates on duty, admissions, deaths and trauma.
Year levelFour levels are available: 1st, 2nd, 3rd and 4th Year. The Residents tab has a button per level showing how many residents it holds; pick one to filter the list.
Add a residentOn the Residents tab. Type the name and choose the year level, then Add resident. Added names are saved on this device, appear in the Resident in charge dropdown immediately, and count in every residents figure.
Most common ESI per residentOn the Residents tab. For each resident it counts visits at every ESI level, works out the level they see most often, and shows their high-acuity share (ESI 1–2 as a percentage of their visits). The mix bar shows the spread across ESI 1 to 5. Ranked by high-acuity share.
Triage filterOn the Print Census tab, ESI 1 to 5 can be chosen individually, or ESI 1–2 together for high-acuity cases.
Top / lowest residentsRanked by visits handled in the selected period. Every roster resident is listed, including those with no visit yet, so the lowest panel shows who has had none. The number on the left of the lowest panel is the overall rank, lowest being 1.
Daily censusAll visits on one consultation date, listed by time, with the resident in charge, plus a rolling 14-day table.
Secondary diagnosisAn additional diagnosis of the same visit, chosen from the same list. Picking "OTHERS (type manually)" opens a box; what you type is recorded as the secondary diagnosis and shown on the Secondary Diagnosis tab under "Secondary typed under OTHERS". Each visit has one primary diagnosis; the secondary one is optional.
OthersVisits whose primary diagnosis is OTHERS. The specific diagnosis must be typed and is listed in the Others tab.
Pediatric sharePatients under 19 years ÷ total visits. Months and days are converted to years.
Diagnosis % Cases of that diagnosis ÷ all visits in the year. Trauma % uses trauma cases as denominator; death % uses all deaths.
Age groups<1, 1–4, 5–12, 13–18, 19–39, 40–59, 60+ years.
