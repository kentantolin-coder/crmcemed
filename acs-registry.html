Standards and time targets used in this registry

  • Troponin TAT — CAP (CMS QCDR CAP9): defined as order in the LIS → verified result in the LIS; target 60 minutes. Excludes point-of-care tests and add-on orders. cap.org
  • Troponin TAT — ACC Chest Pain Center Accreditation: measured arrival → result, percent within 60 minutes. A different start point from CAP; this registry reports both.
  • ESC 2023 ACS: the 0 h/1 h algorithm applies irrespective of local turnaround time — 0 h and 1 h are blood-draw times, not result times. No TAT target is set.
  • Repeat sampling (ACC/AHA 2025): after time zero, hs-cTn at 1–2 h; conventional cTn at 3–6 h.
  • ECG: acquire and interpret within 10 minutes of presentation. Repeat serially if non-diagnostic.
  • Primary PCI: first-medical-contact → device ≤90 min (≤120 min if transfer). Fibrinolysis: FMC → needle ≤30 min.
  • Door-in-door-out for transfer ≤30 min (ESC).
  • Troponin positivity: value above the sex-specific 99th percentile upper reference limit; MI additionally needs a rise/fall plus ischaemia evidence.
  • Numbers from the 2025 ACC/AHA/ACEP/NAEMSP/SCAI ACS guideline, 2023 ESC ACS guideline, and the CAP troponin TAT measure. Confirm against CRMC's own protocol before clinical use.
